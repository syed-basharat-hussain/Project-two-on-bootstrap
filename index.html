Who We Are
Building better, more hopeful lives together.
Umeed Welfare Trust is a community-focused organization dedicated to helping vulnerable and underprivileged people build better and more hopeful lives. Through food assistance, education, healthcare, emergency relief, and community support, we aim to serve people with dignity, compassion, and transparency.
